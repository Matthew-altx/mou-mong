# Mou-mong: Hong Kong temple fortune-stick guide

[Open the free tool in English](https://drawlot-helper.alightlefton.com/draw/?lang=en) · [繁體中文網站](https://drawlot-helper.alightlefton.com/) · [Reading catalogue](https://drawlot-helper.alightlefton.com/fortune-sticks/)

Mou-mong is an independent cultural project created by Matthew Ng in Hong Kong. It helps readers look up traditional fortune-stick texts associated with Man Mo Temple, Wong Tai Sin and Che Kung, and use them as a starting point for reflection.

## What you can do

If you already have a temple name and a stick number, select the temple and enter the number to read the corresponding text. If you are exploring the tradition, you can draw a stick online or start with the [first-time guide](https://drawlot-helper.alightlefton.com/guide/), written in Traditional Chinese.

The tool does not require your name, birthday or private question. Its interface is available in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English, Thai, Korean and Japanese. Cultural articles and source documentation are mainly in Traditional Chinese. Core drawing and number lookup are free.

## Reading a result

Read the traditional text first, then check its source note. Contemporary interpretations are presented separately and are AI-assisted; they have not been reviewed by the temples or independent experts.

A practical next step is to write down what the reading brings to mind, identify information you still need, and choose one small action you can review later. A stick does not establish facts about another person or guarantee a future outcome.

This website is not an official service of any temple. Its content is cultural reference and a prompt for self-reflection; it does not replace medical, legal or financial advice. Online drawing should not be treated as the same experience as visiting a temple.
