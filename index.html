Getting started

From a question to a small next step.

Core drawing and number lookup are free. You do not need to enter your name, birthday or private question.

  1. Choose your starting point

    Already have a stick? Select its temple and enter its number. If you are exploring, draw a stick online or read the first-time guide in Traditional Chinese.

  2. Read the text and its source

    Start with the traditional text. Check its source note before reading the contemporary interpretation, which is presented separately.

  3. Reflect, then choose an action

    Write down what the reading brings to mind and what you still need to find out. Choose one small action you can review later.

The interface is available in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English, Thai, Korean and Japanese.

Reading with context

A prompt for reflection.

Contemporary interpretations are AI-assisted. They have not been reviewed by the temples or independent experts.

A fortune stick does not establish facts about another person or guarantee a future outcome. This material is cultural reference; it does not replace medical, legal or financial advice.

Wumang Fortune is not an official service of any temple. Drawing online should not be treated as the same experience as visiting a temple.
